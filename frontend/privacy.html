🔒 Privacy Policy

We care about your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it. Last updated: August 2026

1. Data We Collect

  • Business profile information (name, description, contact details)
  • Catalog items (products/services you add)
  • Orders placed by your customers
  • Message templates and theme preferences
  • API keys you provide (stored encrypted)

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To operate and provide the WhatsApp Agent Platform service
  • To send automated messages on your behalf via WhatsApp
  • To display your catalog and storefront to customers
  • To generate analytics and reports for your business

3. Data Storage & Security

  • All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256)
  • API keys are stored separately via env vars/secrets
  • Data is stored in SQLite (local) or PostgreSQL (production)
  • We maintain backups and disaster recovery procedures

4. Data Retention & Deletion

You can export your data (Dashboard → Account Health → GDPR Export) or request full deletion at any time. We will permanently delete your data within 7 days of request, except where we need to retain it for legal compliance.

5. Third-Party Services

  • WhatsApp (Meta) — for messaging via unofficial bridge
  • Chart.js — for analytics visualizations (client-side only)
  • Razorpay — for payment processing (only if you configure it)
  • Figma — for design sync (only if you configure it)
  • Google Fonts — for typography

6. Free Beta

The Platform is completely FREE during beta. We do not charge any fees, collect payment information, or require credit card details. Your data will not be sold or monetized.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your data. Contact support@wa-platform.com to exercise these rights.
